# PratibhaScan Standard Assessment Guidelines

This guide details the standard battery of physical and biometric talent tests based on Sports Authority of India (SAI) and international scouting norms.

## Standard Protocols

### 1. 30-Meter Flying Sprint
- **Objective**: Measure maximal sprint velocity and initial acceleration.
- **Equipment**: Laser timing gates or synchronized stopwatches, flat running track.
- **Procedure**: Athlete accelerates from a static start; timing is recorded to 2 decimal places. Best of 3 trials is selected.

### 2. Vertical Jump (Countermovement Jump)
- **Objective**: Measure explosive power of lower extremity musculature.
- **Equipment**: Jump mat or Vertec vane system.
- **Procedure**: Stand flat-footed, jump straight up touching highest reach point without preliminary steps.

### 3. 20-Meter Multi-Stage Shuttle Run (Beep Test)
- **Objective**: Assess cardiorespiratory endurance and estimate VO2 max.
- **Equipment**: 20m flat track marked with cones, audio speaker with standardized cadence.
- **Procedure**: Athlete runs between markers pacing with audio beeps until exhaustion or 2 consecutive failed shuttles.

### 4. Sit and Reach Test
- **Objective**: Measure lower back and hamstring flexibility.
- **Equipment**: Standard Sit & Reach box.
- **Procedure**: Athlete sits with legs extended against box, reaches forward smoothly without jerking.
